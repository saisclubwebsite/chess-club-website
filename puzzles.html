Famous games

Games worth knowing

Classics we go over at the club. Look them up and play through them yourself.

♛

The Opera Game

Paul Morphy, Paris 1858. Played in a box at the opera, and a perfect lesson in developing your pieces fast. Level 6 plays its finish.

♞

Légal's Mate

The trap from our home page: a "free" queen that costs the whole game. Level 4 finishes it.

♝

The Immortal Game

Anderssen vs. Kieseritzky, London 1851. White gives up both rooks, a bishop and the queen, and still wins.
