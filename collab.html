Chess Club × AI Lab

Building this website

This website is a collaboration between the Chess Club and the AI Lab. Chess Club members decided what the site needed: club info, puzzles, the leadership team and our partners. The AI Lab helped turn those ideas into a working site, from the animated chess game on the home page to the puzzle arcade.

Chess Club × Student Council

Hosting school tournaments

The Chess Club regularly partners with the Student Council to host tournaments for the whole school. The Student Council helps organise and promote the events, and the Chess Club runs the boards, the pairings and the games. Together we make it easy for any student to sit down and play.
