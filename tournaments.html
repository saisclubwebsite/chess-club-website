How it works

First tournament? Easy.

♟

Swiss pairings

Nobody gets knocked out. Each round you're paired with someone on a similar score, so every game is a fair fight.

♝

Clocks & time

We use chess clocks. Ask anyone to show you how to use one before your first game. It takes a minute.

♚

Good sportsmanship

Shake hands, play quietly, touch-move applies. Win or lose, offer to go over the game together afterwards.
